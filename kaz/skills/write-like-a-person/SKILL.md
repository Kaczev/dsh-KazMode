# Write like a person

## The one rule underneath all the others

Put yourself in the reader's place for every sentence. Ask: **will this make them stop and work something out?**

A reader stops when a sentence makes them hunt for a subject, re-read a clause, guess what a pronoun points at, or wonder why you're arguing against something they never thought. Every specific habit below is one of those stops.

The test is not "is this accurate" or "is this impressive". It is "can the reader take this in on one pass".

## The twelve habits

### 1. Finish the sentence

Give the subject, the verb, and the object. Complete clauses read smoothly; clipped ones make the reader fill gaps.

- No: 这条线到最后我会回到它。
- Yes: 不过，我们后面再讨论这个。

Two gaps in one nine-character sentence, and the reader has to close both.

### 2. Do not negate something you never raised

If the reader has not thought of it, refuting it makes them stop and look for it.

- No: 这里有一个常见的误解要纠正，它不是说测量的时候把粒子碰跑了。
- Yes: 这句话讲的不是测量的时候把粒子碰跑了，讲的是这种状态本身就不存在。

Test: did the thing you are denying appear in an earlier sentence? If not, it is premature.

### 3. Name it, do not point at it

"This layer", "that line", "this one" all send the reader backwards.

- No: 自然界在这一层本来就不提供。
- Yes: 自然界中，量子就是具有随机性的。

### 4. Do not give abstractions human actions

Nature does not "provide" things. An abstraction cannot offer, refuse, or want.

- No: 是自然界在这一层本来就不提供。
- Yes: 我们无法获得准确信息。

### 5. Keep the negation off the end

A negation parked at the end of a sentence makes the reader wait to learn what is being denied.

- No: 自然界里不存在一个位置和动量都确定的态。
- Yes: 自然界里，位置和动量都确定的态是不存在的。

### 6. Do not narrate your own writing

Say the thing. Do not announce that you are about to say it, or where you will come back to it.

- No: 要讲清楚改掉了什么，得先把原来的样子摆出来。
- No: 不过这里我要先记一笔。
- No: 这条线到最后我会回到它。

### 7. Do not judge on the reader's behalf

Let them draw the conclusion; do not hand it over.

- No: 贝尔指出这个想法和量子力学会给出不同的结果，可以拿实验来判。
- Yes: 贝尔指出，这个想法和量子力学会给出不同的实验结果。

### 8. Skip the metaphor unless it is the source material's own

Invented comparisons cost the reader a decode step and usually buy nothing.

- No: 牛顿在《自然哲学的数学原理》里给了我们一份宇宙的说明书。
- Yes: 牛顿说宇宙遵循以下四条定理。

Keep a borrowed metaphor if it belongs to the subject: Newton's wound-up clock is Newton's, so it stays.

### 9. Use natural connectives, never stock ones

Banned: 首先 / 其次 / 最后一点 / 综上所述 / 值得注意的是 / 不难发现 / 众所周知 / 由此可见 / 换言之 / 赋能 / 抓手 / 赛道 / 维度.

Fine: 不过 / 所以 / 也就是说 / 十年之后 / 这里我要说明一点 / 现在说开头提到的那个.

If you use 换言之 or 换句话说, make sure the second phrasing really is a different angle. Usually it is the same sentence with more words.

### 10. Vary the sentence length

A run of short sentences reads like output from a machine. Long and short alternating reads like a person.

- Machine: 这是一个短句的例子。
- Person: 这是一个长短句交替的例子。

### 11. Mark where the reader should turn the page

In a script or spoken text, the reader cannot guess when the slide changes. Put the cue on its own line: `【翻到第 N 页】`. Do not head sections with slide numbers like "第 1—2 页"; that is scaffolding for the writer, not information for the reader.

### 12. Do not end by summarising

AI likes to say everything and then say it again. A recap tells the reader nothing is coming, so attention drops. End with an action: answer the question you planted earlier, land the consequence, give the last step. Not a restatement.

## Self-check pass

After drafting, run this and fix what it finds. Do not skip it: these habits survive a careful first draft, because they feel like clarity while you are writing.

1. **Count the sentences under six characters.** Any that are not deliberate emphasis: expand them.
2. **Search for the stock connectives** in habit 9. Replace each.
3. **Backwards-reference sweep.** For every 这 / 那 / 它 / 该, check that its referent is in the previous sentence or two and unambiguous.
4. **Check every negation.** For each 不是 / 不 / 没有, ask whether the thing being denied was already on the page.
5. **Hunt abstraction-as-agent.** Look for an abstract noun doing a human verb.
6. **Read the ending.** If it restates the body, cut it and write an action instead.
7. **Read it out loud in your head.** Where you stumble, the reader stumbles.

Report what you changed. Do not silently fix and hand back.

## When the text is meant to be spoken

Timing is a hard constraint, not a target. A speaking rate of 220 Chinese characters per minute is a good default. Count the actual characters of the spoken text only — strip stage directions, page cues, and markdown — and state the resulting duration. Do not estimate the length by feel; the estimate is wrong far more often than the count.

If a requested structure does not fit the time budget, say so with the numbers and offer explicit options. Do not quietly compress what the user asked for, and do not quietly exceed the limit.

Leave cut points for the speaker: mark the sentences that can be dropped without breaking the argument, and say how much time each one buys.

## Where these came from

These habits were worked out by the user across several rounds of correcting machine-written drafts. Every rule here has a before-and-after pair taken from a real correction, so the examples are the evidence, not illustrations. Treat them as settled preferences, not suggestions to weigh against your own sense of style.
